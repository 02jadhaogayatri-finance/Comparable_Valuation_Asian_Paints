# 📊 Comparable Company Analysis – Asian Paints

This project presents a relative valuation of Asian Paints using Comparable Company Analysis (Trading Comps). The objective is to estimate the fair value of the company by benchmarking it against industry peers.

## 🚀 Objective
To evaluate whether Asian Paints is overvalued or undervalued by comparing its valuation multiples with similar companies in the paint industry.

## 🏢 Company Overview
Asian Paints is one of the leading paint companies in India, operating in decorative and industrial coatings with a strong market presence.

## 📈 Methodology

### 1. Peer Group Selection
Selected comparable companies within the same industry:
- Berger Paints  
- Kansai Nerolac  
- Indigo Paints  
- Sirca Paints  
- Other listed peers

### 2. Valuation Multiples Used
- EV/EBITDA  
- P/E (Price to Earnings)  
- EV/Sales  

These multiples help compare valuation across companies with similar operations.

### 3. Benchmarking Approach
- Calculated valuation multiples for each peer  
- Derived **median multiples** to avoid outliers  
- Applied these multiples to Asian Paints’ financials  

## 📊 Key Findings

- Median EV/EBITDA: ~15.87x  
- Median P/E: ~28.30x  
- Implied Share Price Range: ~₹1,150  

📌 Current Market Price: ~₹2,485  

## 📉 Conclusion

Asian Paints appears **overvalued** compared to its peer group based on relative valuation metrics.
